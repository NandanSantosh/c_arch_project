FORMULA
Weighted Cost = sum(Execution Count x Assumed Instruction Latency)
Optimistic, Moderate, and Conservative use different vector latencies.
These are weighted latency-based estimates, not measured CPU cycles.
The calculation does not model pipelining, instruction overlap,
resource conflicts, dependencies, or pipeline stalls.
